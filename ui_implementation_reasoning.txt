UI Implementation Reasoning
============================

Architecture
------------
The existing Flask, SQLite, repository, service, and Jinja2 structure remains
the application foundation. New features use the existing blueprints and data
access patterns. Database initialization remains in app/database.py, separate
from request handlers. No Python dependencies were added.

Persistence and account names
-----------------------------
The SQLite schema advances to version 3. The migration adds a per-user
display_name and a messages table, while preserving existing records and
backfilling account names from email addresses. Patient account names are
independent of doctor-managed patient-record names, as requested. Existing
settings submissions without the new optional field continue to work.

Patient History and Analysis
----------------------------
History is now an all-time measurement log with pressure-threshold validity,
recording/confirmation status, and a CSV download. Validity and recording
status are deliberately separate: a measurement is valid when systolic and
diastolic pressures are both below the patient's assigned thresholds.
History no longer contains charts or comparison.

Analysis owns time-range controls, blood-pressure, symptom, and pulse charts,
period statistics, and a comparison table. It retains the required existing
periods (7 days, 14 days, 1 month, and 6 months) and adds the prototype's
30-day option. Statistics are calculated separately for systolic pressure,
diastolic pressure, and pulse. Standard deviation uses the population formula;
its percentage is standard deviation divided by the absolute mean. Mean
variation is the percentage change from the preceding period of equal length;
the absolute change is also displayed. Mini-bars provide a visual comparison
of current and previous-period statistics. Missing comparison data is shown
without inventing a value.

The existing grade and monitoring calculations are unchanged. Grade thresholds
remain those in the pre-existing specifications: Grade 1 starts at the
personalized threshold, Grade 2 at 160/100, and Grade 3 at 180/110. Red
triangles on the charts mark each pressure that reaches its personal threshold.
Symptom intensity labels now describe all values from 0 to 5. Symptom images
are local Lucide SVG assets, with their license included, so icon loading does
not require a CDN.

Doctor experience and messaging
-------------------------------
Unread alerts are ordered by grade descending and then emission time ascending,
so higher-severity alerts take priority and older alerts of equal severity
appear first. Alert links open the corresponding patient's analytics. Archives
navigation is replaced by a searchable Patients page; doctor patient records,
notes, chart history, and measurement comparisons remain available.

Patient-doctor messages are stored in the shared SQLite database and scoped to
the linked patient profile. Routes enforce the existing patient/doctor role
checks and doctor ownership checks. Message bodies are trimmed, required, and
limited to 2,000 characters.

Navigation, appearance, and localization
----------------------------------------
Authenticated navigation is fixed at the bottom and includes the new patient
Measure and Analysis destinations, as well as Messages to preserve the
pre-existing messaging requirement. Doctor navigation offers Home, Patients,
Messages, and Settings. Bootstrap styling uses the template's blue palette,
rounded controls, and panel styling across tables and buttons. New visible
labels, status text, time ranges, and symptom adjectives are translated in
English and French. Light/dark theme behavior is retained.

Verification
------------
The test suite covers schema upgrades, CSV output and validity, statistical
calculations, time-range rendering, messages, patient search, alert ordering,
account-name separation, and local symptom icons. The existing suite also
continues to cover authentication, risk assessment, measurement workflow, and
doctor record ownership.
