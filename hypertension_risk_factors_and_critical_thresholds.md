# Hypertension Risk Factors and Critical Blood Pressure Thresholds

This document defines the critical blood pressure thresholds (in mmHg) based on the patient's cardiovascular risk profile.

## 1. Baseline Threshold (No Risk Factors)
* **Critical Threshold:** **140 / 90 mmHg**
* Applies when none of the moderate or high-risk factors listed below are present.

---

## 2. Moderate Risk Factors
If **at least one** moderate risk factor is present, the critical threshold is lowered to **135 / 85 mmHg**:

* **Advanced age:** $\ge 60$ years old
* **Overweight:** $25 \le \text{BMI} < 30$ *(see BMI calculation note below)*
* **High alcohol consumption:** More than 2 standard drinks per week
* **Smoking:** Current smoker
* **Pregnancy** *(Note: Gestational hypertension requires specific clinical monitoring)*

---

## 3. High Risk Factors
If **at least one** high-risk factor is present, the critical threshold is lowered to **130 / 80 mmHg**:

* **Obesity:** $\text{BMI} \ge 30$ *(mutually exclusive with Overweight)*
* **Cardiovascular disease** (established heart disease)
* **History of stroke** (previous CVA/TIA)
* **Family history** of premature heart disease or stroke
* **Chronic kidney disease (CKD)**
* **Diabetes:** Type 1 or Type 2

---

## 4. Risk Factor Combinations
When a patient presents with multiple risk factors, apply the following cumulative rules:

* **$\ge 3$ Moderate Risk Factors:** The critical threshold drops to **130 / 80 mmHg**.
* **$\ge 2$ High Risk Factors** *OR* **(1 High Risk Factor + $\ge 2$ Moderate Risk Factors):** The critical threshold drops to **125 / 75 mmHg** (minimum floor).

---

## 5. Calculation Rules & Clinical Notes

* **Body Mass Index (BMI) Formula:**
  $$\text{BMI} = \frac{\text{Weight (kg)}}{\text{Height (m)}^2}$$
* **Mutual Exclusivity (Overweight vs. Obesity):** A patient cannot be classified as both overweight and obese simultaneously. If a patient has a $\text{BMI} \ge 30$, they are counted as having **one high-risk factor only** (Obesity), and **not** one high-risk factor plus one moderate risk factor.
